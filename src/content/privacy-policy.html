Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

SodCalc does not collect any personal information from visitors. We do not have user accounts, comment sections, contact forms, or any other mechanism for collecting personal data.

2. Cookies and Tracking

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data such as pages visited, time on site, and browser type. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and, in the future, for displaying advertisements. These services may use their own cookies and data collection practices. We do not control these third-party services and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Storage

Any data you enter into our calculators (such as lawn area or grass type) is processed entirely in your browser. No calculation data is sent to our servers or stored by us.

Checklist checkbox states are saved in your browser's localStorage and remain on your device only. You can clear this data by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the domain registrar listed in the WHOIS record for sodcalc.com.
